01 / The Problem PhishGuard AI Pitch

Modern Scams Are No Longer Obviously Fake

Attackers have evolved from crude grammatical errors to high-context, psychologically tuned social engineering.

01 • THREAT PATTERN

Contextual Deception

Scams imitate actual university exam circulars, campus placement drives, bank KYC updates, and family WhatsApp emergencies with realistic branding.

✕ High visual authenticity
✕ Exploits authority and trust
✕ Targets student vulnerabilities
⚠️ Victims cannot discern fake offers from official notices.
02 • DELIVERY VECTOR

Multi-Channel Infiltration

Threats bypass traditional corporate email filters by arriving via WhatsApp messages, SMS, cropped screenshots, short links, and social chat apps.

✕ Arrives directly on mobile
✕ Zero gateway email inspection
✕ Ephemeral links avoid blacklists
⚠️ Consumer messaging platforms lack native inspection firewalls.
03 • DEFENSE VOID

Verification Vacuum

Everyday users lack enterprise-level security operations centers (SOC). Decisions are made using guesswork, emotional stress, and panic under artificial countdowns.

✕ No immediate second opinion
✕ FOMO and panic override caution
✕ Irreversible UPI / card transfers
⚠️ By the time a user suspects fraud, the money is already gone.
